This protects a free rendering - not a literal rendering of an English figure or set phrase that reads as translated.
- A correct word swapped for a near-synonym only because it is closer to the English or has a slightly different shade. "Fits better", "more natural" or "slightly" is not a reason. But a word in a different sense is not a shade - flag it however small the difference looks ("even now" for "still" meaning "nevertheless"; "strip" for "bundle"). And flag a word when the English pairs it with another on the page ("shallow" and "depth" - "without depth", "depth").

- An English word in brackets after a Sadhguru concept, a coined or technical name, a foreign custom, product or drug, or a pun - (Grace), (consecration), (pendulum), (Marlboro man). - Two English sentences given as one {{LANGUAGE}} sentence, or one as two, when nothing is lost; or an order that is one of several natural {{LANGUAGE}} orders.
- Spacing, paragraph breaks and stray quote marks on their own.
- Anything whose Version 1 would read the same as the original.
- A literal sentence that is natural {{LANGUAGE}} ("that is about you"), and a pronoun the translation has already resolved to its noun.
- A reflexive verb form for something that happens to one's own body ("breaking one's bones").
