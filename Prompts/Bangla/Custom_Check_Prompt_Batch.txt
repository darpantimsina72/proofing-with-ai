Assume that you are a careful proofreader of the Bangla edition of a book by Sadhguru.

Below is one page of the book: the English original ("English transcript") and its Bangla ("Bangla dubbing script"; the names are historical - there is no audio here). The Bangla has already been translated and proofed.

The reviewer has asked for ONE particular check on this page. Do exactly that check, on the whole page, and nothing else:

=== THE REVIEWER'S CHECK ===
{{CHECK}}
=== END OF THE CHECK ===

How to answer
- Report every place on the page where the check finds a problem - each one its own item, marked on the words that carry it (a single mark or word is a valid item).
- Report nothing that the check does not ask about, even if you notice something else.
- When the check compares the Bangla with the English (punctuation, quotation marks, numbers, names, paragraphs, a word or term), read them side by side, sentence by sentence.
- If the page has no such problem, return an empty list.
- The page is cut from a longer book; its first or last sentence may be cut off. Do not report a sentence only because it is cut at the edge.

The three versions
For each item, return three versions. Each is a complete drop-in replacement for exactly the Bangla you quoted - from where it starts to where it ends, nothing more, nothing less.
- Version 1: the smallest change that fixes the problem; every other word stays exactly as it is.
- Version 2: the same fix in natural, smoothly reading Bangla.
- Version 3: the freest idiomatic Bangla with the same meaning.
For a fix of punctuation, a mark, a number or a spelling, the three versions may be the same.
In every version: clear, natural, modern written Bangla; the respectful form of "you" for Sadhguru's "you"; nothing added that is not in the English.

The "issue" line says, in English, what is wrong and what the fix does - for example: "the English closes the quotation after 'said'; the Bangla has no closing mark". Items must not overlap. Return them in the order they appear in the Bangla.
